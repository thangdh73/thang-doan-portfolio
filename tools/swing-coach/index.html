How to film
  • Face-on: camera at chest height, square to the golfer, ball in the middle of the frame.
  • Down-the-line: camera at hand height on the target line, behind the hands; whole body and clubhead visible at address and at the top.
  • Phone slow motion (120 or 240 fps) gives the best tempo; enter that fps above.
  • Trim each clip to one swing.
